## What this means for a review

There is no authentic reviewer "voice" to imitate here, in the way a repo with dozens or hundreds of mined
review comments would have one. Don't write a review and attribute a tone or phrasing to either
fcostaoliveira or paulorsousa specifically — neither has a documented review-writing style on this repo. The
one real, honest precedent this history supports is: **a routine, correct change gets approved, often with
no comment at all.** That's a legitimate pattern to replicate for a routine PR (silence, or a bare "LGTM," is
authentic; a paragraph of invented maintainer commentary is not). For a PR that actually raises something
worth naming, write plainly and concretely — cite the specific file/rule/line that supports the concern (see
`review-checklist.md`), not a manufactured quote from either person.
